Interactive Game · Body Tracking

Slice /
In Motion

A camera-based slicing game that turns full-body movement into the controller. Move fast, slice the characters, avoid the bombs, and climb the local leaderboard.

How to play

Step into frame with your upper body and both arms visible. Swing either hand quickly through a character to slice it.

Tracking

Full-body pose estimation follows both hands in real time. Slow movement is ignored so the gesture feels intentional.

Built with

p5.js · ml5.js · BlazePose · Web Audio API
